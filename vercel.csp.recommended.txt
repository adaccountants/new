Recommended starter Content-Security-Policy — NOT applied.

Do not enable this until it has been tested against the live site. A naive CSP
will break inline JSON-LD <script> tags, Framer Motion, and possibly other
inline styles/scripts this app uses.

Suggested starting point (adjust after watching the browser console):

  Content-Security-Policy:
    default-src 'self';
    base-uri 'self';
    object-src 'none';
    frame-ancestors 'none';
    form-action 'self';
    script-src 'self' 'unsafe-inline';
    style-src 'self' 'unsafe-inline';
    img-src 'self' data: blob: https://bglpwknowjvhuyykuuww.supabase.co;
    font-src 'self' data:;
    connect-src 'self' https://bglpwknowjvhuyykuuww.supabase.co wss://bglpwknowjvhuyykuuww.supabase.co;
    media-src 'self' https://bglpwknowjvhuyykuuww.supabase.co;
    frame-src 'none';
    upgrade-insecure-requests;

Why these directives:
- script-src 'unsafe-inline': JSON-LD <script type="application/ld+json"> in the
  document head, plus any other inline scripts the Vite/TanStack Start runtime
  may emit. A nonce/hash based policy is better long-term but needs a pass that
  inventories every inline script.
- style-src 'unsafe-inline': Tailwind/runtime and Framer Motion inline styles.
- img-src + media-src: CMS card images and knowledge files live on the public
  Supabase storage host.
- connect-src: browser Supabase client (REST, Auth, Realtime websocket).
- frame-ancestors 'none': same intent as X-Frame-Options: DENY.

After enabling, click through home, services, knowledge downloads, contact
submit, and /admin login to confirm nothing is blocked.
